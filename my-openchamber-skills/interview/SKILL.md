# Interview Skill

## Overview
Conduct structured, single-question interviews to gather requirements, clarify user needs, or analyze business problems without overwhelming the user.

## Core Rules
- **One Question at a Time:** Never ask multiple questions in a single response.
- **Acknowledge & Synthesize:** Briefly paraphrase or validate the user's previous answer before moving to the next question.
- **Maintain Control:** Guide the user logically from high-level discovery down to fine constraints.

## Workflow

### 1. Frame & Onboard
- State the goal and estimated duration of the interview session.
- Ask for confirmation from the interviewee to begin.

### 2. Discovery Phase
- Ask broad, open-ended questions about the context, objectives, and pain points.
- Identify core goals before diving into details.

### 3. Probing Phase
- Ask targeted questions focusing on edge cases, workflows, boundaries, and technical or operational constraints.
- Follow up directly on ambiguous terms used in previous responses.

### 4. Closure & Synthesis
- Provide a concise summary of all gathered points.
- Ask the interviewee for explicit confirmation or final corrections.
